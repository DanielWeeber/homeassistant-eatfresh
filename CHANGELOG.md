# Changelog

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-25)

- Servings: the recipe view of the collection has a counter (1 to 99); the amounts of the ingredients are recalculated. New option "default servings" (1 to 12, default 3; existing setups get 3). A recipe opens with the default when its `yields` in KitchenOwl is 2 to 8; with 1 or more than 8 (often "1 cake" or "12 pieces") it opens with its own number; with no `yields`, or with a start value above 99, there is no counter and every amount stays character for character (a `servings` sent to it is dropped everywhere: list, planning, week, actions). The choice is not remembered: each recipe opens with the start value again. Factor 1 changes nothing. `servings` is a whole number from 1 to 99: the actions also take `3.0`, the WebSocket commands do not; `2.5`, `true` and `"3"` are refused.
- Rounding (one place, `portions.py`): g and ml in steps (1 below 20, 5 below 250, 10 below 1000, 50 from 1000; between 250 and 1000 an exact multiple of 5 stays), kg and l in 0.05 steps (below 1 they become g or ml), spoons, cups (Tasse) and the other known units (mg, cl, dl, cm, mm, drops) in halves (quarters below 1, at least 0.25), pinches whole. Things that cannot be split (can, pack, tub, jar, bunch, clove, egg, piece without a unit, ...) are rounded up to whole pieces, never below 1; up to 0.1 above a whole number is rounded down (2.08 becomes 2, 2.2 becomes 3). Where the whole number differs from the exact one by more than 0.01, the panel shows "calculated" with the exact number next to the amount. Read as amounts: ranges (`2-3`), fractions (`1/2`, `½`), `ca.`, a decimal comma between digits (`1,5 kg`; a comma followed by a space separates parts), a dot with three digits ending in 0 as thousands (`1.000 g`, `1.250 g`; not before kg and l), a unit without a number (`Dose`, `Bund`, `EL`) as 1, written with its number (`3 Bund`; a pinch stays `Prise`), a single unknown word after the number as pieces (`3 Eier`, `1 Große`). More words after a known unit stay as written, only the number changes (`2 EL gehäuft`, `1 Prise Salz`). Text EatFresh cannot read as an amount stays as it is.
- The recipe in KitchenOwl stays unchanged: EatFresh writes neither `yields` nor ingredients because of the servings. The shopping list gets the recalculated amounts (also for ingredients EatFresh remembers that are missing in KitchenOwl).
- Week plan: each entry with a day has its own servings and a counter. KitchenOwl stores them per entry (`yields`); it does not change an existing entry, so changing the number removes the entry and creates it again (with the old number again if that fails). If the same recipe is in the plan without a day as well, the number cannot be changed (`unscheduled_entry`, nothing is written): KitchenOwl would drop that entry when creating the other one. If creating fails and creating the entry again with the old number fails too, the error is `plan_entry_lost`. Planning always sends a number when the recipe has one. The transfer of the week to the shopping list uses the number of each entry; changing a number afterwards does not touch the list (the panel says so). A recipe opened from the week plan opens with the number of the entry.
- Actions: `eatfresh.plan_recipe` and `eatfresh.add_recipe_to_shopping_list` take `servings` (1 to 99, optional); `eatfresh.get_planner` returns `servings` per entry and per recipe without a day. The sensor "Today" has `servings` in `recipes` and in `week` (the entry's number, else the start value, else null).
- Smaller package at Picnic: when the last package is needed for at most 60 % of its content, the shopping tab suggests a smaller package for the rest. The choice is remembered like the main product (`small` in `picnic_map`), has its own quantity, and goes into the cart after the main product; if the second call fails, the first stays marked as sent and the error is reported (`maybe_sent` if unclear); "Undo" takes out both. A count with a pack unit on the list (`2 Dose`) now means that many packs of the product; for a product counted in pieces (`6 Stück`) it is not divided by the pieces (only a multipack such as `3 x 200 g` divides). With a fixed quantity remembered for the ingredient there is no suggestion at all (`rest` is `null`, `small_amount` 0). EatFresh keeps the exact counts of what it put on the list (`list_exact`, 14 days, at most 500, dropped when the quantity on the list no longer matches or the article is gone) to find the rest; a list changed by hand gives no suggestion.
- Shopping tab with categories: entries are grouped (14 groups in the order of a supermarket, own groups of KitchenOwl before "Other"). Order of choice: the user's own choice (`shop_departments`), the KitchenOwl category (by `default_key`, else by name), else EatFresh sorts by the name (German and English, about 1000 words). The tab is now a plain list to tick off without the Picnic integration. An entry without a product shows a magnifier ("Choose product" on wide screens) instead of a line below it; on a partial success the header counts entries.
- Ticking off takes the entry off the KitchenOwl list at once (the list is read first; only entries on it are taken). It stays 3 days under "Done" with "Back on the list" (adds it again with the remembered amount text, amounts add up; `item_gone` if the article was deleted in the meantime). Entries in the Picnic cart leave the list 3 days after sending, but only while the amount text on the list is still the one sent (not when sending was unclear); this runs when the tab is opened and before a transfer to the list, never by the clock. If the text changed (a later transfer added to it), the entry is open again at once: `eatfresh/shopping` returns it without `sent_at` and with `cart_hint` (the text sent), and the panel shows "Already in the cart: 250 g" and does not preselect the entry for the next send; after 3 days only the mark goes, the entry stays. Marks of 0.4.x have no amount text and never take an entry off the list; they go after 3 days. The "in the cart" marks now live 3 days instead of 14.
- Categories are written to KitchenOwl (new option "write categories", default on): when the user moves an entry, and after a transfer to the list for the transferred articles that have no category (at most 40, one after the other, errors only in the log; the transfer never waits for it or fails because of it). EatFresh never overwrites a category an article already has by itself, creates a missing category (searched first; the categories of the household are cached for 5 minutes), and never renames or deletes one. Moving an entry to "Other" clears the category in KitchenOwl. The option off stops every write of categories.
- New WebSocket commands: `eatfresh/planner/set_servings`, `eatfresh/shopping/check`, `eatfresh/shopping/uncheck`, `eatfresh/shopping/set_department`, `eatfresh/picnic/map_small`. Changed: `eatfresh/collection/recipe` (`servings` in, `portions` and a `portion` per ingredient out), `eatfresh/shopping_list/add` and `eatfresh/planner/add` (`servings` in), `eatfresh/planner` (`default_servings`, `servings` and `base` per entry), `eatfresh/shopping` (`departments`, `done`, `done_days`; per entry `department`, `cart_hint`, `rest`, `exact` (only when the number on the list differs from the exact one), `small`, `small_amount`; `amount` may be 0 when `small_amount` is 1 or more), `eatfresh/picnic/search` (`rest`, `main_unit_quantity` in, `fits_rest` per hit), `eatfresh/picnic/send` (`small_amount` in and in `sent`). `eatfresh/shopping/set_department` takes the name from the list (only an entry on it can be moved). New error codes: `not_on_list` (check, set_department), `item_gone` and `not_done` (uncheck), `unknown_department`, `unscheduled_entry` and `plan_entry_lost` (set_servings).
- Store version 1.5: the sections `list_exact`, `shop_done` and `shop_departments` are added to existing files without losing data. Going back to 0.4.1 loads the store, but 0.4.1 drops marks that hold only a smaller package, does not take the smaller package out on "Undo", and drops the ticked-off list, the own groups and the exact counts on its next save.
- **Changed behaviour:** without `servings`, the actions and the transfer of the week now recalculate the amounts to the start value (the default servings, usually 3) of the recipe. Before, the amounts of the recipe went to the list unchanged. A recipe without `yields` is not changed.
- **Changed behaviour:** EatFresh now takes entries off the KitchenOwl shopping list: ticked entries at once, entries in the Picnic cart 3 days after sending (only if their amount text is unchanged). It still never deletes an article or a recipe in KitchenOwl (the only DELETE calls are for a meal plan entry and for an entry of the shopping list).

## 0.4.1 (2026-11-19)

- Ingredients do not get lost any more when an article is deleted in KitchenOwl (KitchenOwl then takes it out of every recipe). EatFresh keeps its own copy of the ingredients of every recipe (store version 1.4, new section `ingredients`; existing files get an empty section and learn the current state on first sight). The copy is learned from answers EatFresh gets anyway (the recipe list, a single recipe, a recipe EatFresh creates), with no extra request to KitchenOwl. A change made in KitchenOwl wins: a renamed article, a changed amount, a new ingredient. An ingredient that is gone there stays in EatFresh and counts as missing. Swapping an ingredient for another one in the recipe editor of KitchenOwl counts as one removed and one added, so the old one shows as missing until "Remove from recipe" is used. The copy of a recipe is only dropped when the recipe has not been in any recipe list for more than 30 days; an empty list never drops anything. If KitchenOwl gives a recipe number to a new recipe (its `created_at` differs), the copy starts again from the new recipe. At most 300 ingredients per recipe and 3000 recipes are remembered.
- The collection, the recipe view and the search (`eatfresh/collection`, `eatfresh/collection/recipe`, `eatfresh.search_collection`) show and find missing ingredients too. `eatfresh/collection` has `missing` (a number) per recipe, `eatfresh/collection/recipe` has `missing` (the names).
- New WebSocket commands `eatfresh/collection/restore` (puts the missing ingredients back into the KitchenOwl recipe; answers `restored` and `missing`) and `eatfresh/collection/forget` (EatFresh forgets missing ingredients, all or the given `names`; KitchenOwl is not changed; answers `removed` and `missing`). The recipe view of the panel shows a notice with "Restore" and "Remove from recipe" when ingredients are missing, and marks them in the list.
- Before the ingredients of a recipe go to the shopping list, missing ones are restored in KitchenOwl, so the list is complete. If that fails, the transfer of that recipe stops with an error. This also holds for the week plan and for the action `eatfresh.add_to_shopping_list`, where no button is pressed, and for an ingredient somebody took out of the recipe in KitchenOwl on purpose: EatFresh cannot tell that from a deleted article and puts it back. "Remove from recipe" in the recipe view makes EatFresh forget it for good.
- Restoring only adds: the recipe is read right before, the request holds every ingredient it had, unchanged, plus the missing ones, and the answer is checked. If the read fails, nothing is written. Restores run one after the other. EatFresh writes only for the button or a transfer to the shopping list and still never deletes articles or recipes in KitchenOwl.
- Reading ingredients, all sources: a name is not split at a comma when the noun only comes after it ("50 g gemahlene, geschälte Mandeln (blanchiert)" keeps its name; all other lines, also English ones, split as before); "Päck.", "Päckchen", "Pkg." are read as the unit "Pck."; "Ei(er)" and "Zwiebel(n)" become singular only for the amount 1 without a unit, else plural ("1 kg Kartoffel(n)" is "Kartoffeln"); "nach Geschmack", "nach Belieben" and "nach Bedarf" in front of a name become a note; "Salz und Pfeffer" without an amount becomes two ingredients; notes with brackets inside brackets keep their reading order.
- Reading ingredients, Chefkoch: a note no longer starts with a comma ("200 g Zucker, , feiner"), and an adjective after the noun moves in front of it ("Linsen, rote" becomes "rote Linsen").
- Going back to 0.4.0 keeps favorites, hidden recipes and the Picnic data, but 0.4.0 drops the remembered ingredients on its next save.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-16)

- Shopping list to Picnic: new WebSocket commands `eatfresh/shopping` (the KitchenOwl shopping list with the remembered Picnic product, a suggested quantity (for a sent entry the quantity in the cart), `skip`, `sent_at` and `sent_uncertain` per entry, plus `picnic: {available, search, reason, reason_code}`; reads only KitchenOwl and the store, never Picnic), `eatfresh/picnic/search` (at most 60 characters, up to 8 products with `id`, `name`, `unit_quantity`, `price` in cents), `eatfresh/picnic/map`, `eatfresh/picnic/unmap`, `eatfresh/picnic/skip`, `eatfresh/picnic/send` (up to 60 entries, 1 to 20 pieces each) and `eatfresh/picnic/undo` (`remove: false` only forgets the mark). New error codes `picnic_unavailable` and `picnic_error`. Reasons, failed entries (`failed[].code`) and errors (`translation_key` `picnic_<code>`) carry stable codes; the panel shows them in German or English, the English `message` stays as a fallback.
- EatFresh uses the Picnic integration of Home Assistant, with no credentials, no requirements and no manifest dependency of its own (an `after_dependencies` entry would make Home Assistant install the Picnic library whenever EatFresh loads, and a failed install would stop EatFresh; the Picnic entry is found at run time instead). All access sits in `picnic.py` and checks every assumption (entry loaded, client and its methods present, form of the search hits; both the object form of python-picnic-api2 2.x and the list form of 1.x are read). Without the Picnic integration EatFresh runs as before and `eatfresh/shopping` answers with `available: false`.
- Products go into the cart through the client of the Picnic integration (`add_product`), one after the other with a pause of one second from the end of one call to the start of the next, each call with a time limit of 15 seconds. The action `picnic.add_product` is not used: it throws Picnic's answer away. Only entries that are on the shopping list and not marked as sent are sent; a lock stops a second transfer at the same time (`busy`). An entry only counts as in the cart when the cart in Picnic's answer shows the product with the expected quantity (pieces that were in the cart before are counted in). Before the first product a run reads the cart once through the client (`get_cart`), so the pieces before are known for every product; after that only the answers of the single calls count. If that read fails or its answer cannot be read, the run does not start: nothing is marked or sent, and the error carries the code `cart_unreadable` (a rejected login keeps `auth`). A product whose own line in the cart shows no quantity is not sent (code `cart_unreadable`); other lines without a quantity (for example a deposit) do not block the rest. Calls to Picnic per run: one read of the cart plus one call per entry sent.
- The run stops at the first error of any kind, with no further call to Picnic: a read error (an error page without JSON), a broken connection, a timeout, a rejected login, an answer in an unknown form, or a product Picnic did not put in (an error object, or a cart the same as before in every number, which the Picnic library returns without an error; code `not_added`). If the cart changed but does not show the product with the expected quantity (for example under another id than in the search), the entry is "maybe in the cart" (`maybe_sent`), never `not_added`, so a second send cannot add it twice. The entries after it are reported with the code `not_tried` and are not tried. The Picnic library checks no HTTP status, so there is no detection of 403 or 429 any more (code `blocked` removed; it could never happen).
- Before each call the entry is saved at once as "maybe in the cart" (`sent_uncertain`); Picnic's answer turns it into "in the cart" or removes it again (surely not in the cart). After a timeout, an unclear answer or an unexpected quantity it stays "maybe in the cart" (code `maybe_sent`) and is never sent again by itself. Marks are written to disk at once, not after a delay.
- After a timeout the blocking call goes on in the background; the next call to Picnic (search, add or undo) waits until it really ended, at most 30 seconds, else it is not sent (code `call_running`). The message of `call_running` says to restart Home Assistant if waiting does not help. A new search is refused while one runs (code `search_running`). A failed search is not repeated by itself. After a transfer without errors the Picnic integration is asked once to read its cart again.
- Undo reads the cart once (`get_cart`), then takes the sent quantity out through the client of the Picnic integration (there is no action for it) and removes the mark. It compares the pieces before and after: if they did not drop by the sent quantity, the entry becomes "maybe in the cart" (`maybe_sent`). If the cart holds fewer pieces than were sent, nothing is taken out and the entry becomes "maybe in the cart" (code `cart_changed`). If the read fails, nothing is taken out and the mark stays (`cart_unreadable`). It is only possible for "in the cart": for "maybe in the cart" `eatfresh/picnic/undo` refuses (`maybe_no_undo`), and only `remove: false` ("Not in the cart") forgets the mark. If the answer to undo is unclear, the entry becomes "maybe in the cart", so nothing is taken out twice. Nothing is ever ordered and no delivery slot is booked.
- `eatfresh/shopping`: `picnic.available` is false (reason `unknown_form`) when the client of the Picnic integration has no `add_product` or no `get_cart`; the action `picnic.add_product` is no longer needed (reason `no_action` removed).
- `eatfresh.search_collection` takes `name_only` (default `false`): with `true` the query only matches the recipe name, not the ingredients. The response is unchanged.
- The suggested quantity comes from the amount on the list and the pack size (g, kg, ml, l, pieces, multipacks like `4 x 250g`, decimal comma); different kinds of quantity or unreadable text give 1.
- Store version 1.3: `picnic_map`, `picnic_skip` and `picnic_sent` are added to existing files without losing data. At most 500 remembered products, 500 excluded ingredients and 300 marks; marks older than 14 days or of entries no longer on the list are dropped.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-15)

- New actions with a response, for scripts and voice assistants: `eatfresh.search_collection` (search the collection by name or ingredient, with the planned days), `eatfresh.get_planner` (meal plan from a day on 1 to 31 days, plus recipes without a day), `eatfresh.plan_recipe` and `eatfresh.unplan_recipe`. Only recipes of the own collection are accepted (`not_in_collection`); KitchenOwl errors and a rejected token behave as in the other actions. The calendar and the sensor are updated right after planning or removing.
- The meal plan is fetched every minute instead of every 15 minutes, so a change made in the KitchenOwl app shows up after about a minute. The interval can be set in the options (1 to 60 minutes); existing setups get one minute. Each fetch is one request to KitchenOwl, and a plan loaded for the cards counts as the fetch of that minute.
- Sensor "Today" has a new attribute `week`: the meals from today to six days ahead (`date`, `recipe_id`, `name`), sorted by date. It moves on shortly after midnight.
- The recipe list from KitchenOwl now also keeps the names of the ingredients (used by the search of the collection).
- Sensor "Today" no longer writes a new state every minute when a recipe with a photo is planned for today. A signed image address is reused until less than 12 of its 24 hours are left, so the state only changes with the plan (and at most twice a day for a new address). The sensor signs for Home Assistant's content user. The panel and the cards get the same address for the same photo as well, so the browser cache works.
- Calls that need the meal plan at the same time (for example many `eatfresh.get_planner` with an old plan) share one request to KitchenOwl.
- `eatfresh.search_collection` takes `limit` (1 to 200, default 50) and answers `total`, the number of all matches. If only the meal plan cannot be loaded, it still answers, with empty `planned_dates`; a rejected token still fails.
- An unknown `recipe_id` loads the recipe list from KitchenOwl at most every 5 seconds, not on every try.
- A recipe opened with the "Recipe link" button (or a link in the search field) is saved to the collection by itself: the panel calls `eatfresh/favorite` once after loading. The notice "Saved to your collection." shows the suggested categories, with "Change" and "Undo"; "Undo" takes the heart off and hides the recipe (it stays in KitchenOwl). A recipe that was in the collection already gives "Was already in your collection." (a hidden one is shown again) and nothing to undo. If saving fails, the recipe stays visible with a message and "Try again". Reloading the page and going back save nothing again. Recipes from search results are still saved only with the heart.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-15)

- "Recipe link" button in the top right corner of the panel; the field at the bottom is gone.
- Open recipes from the collection. They are shown as stored in KitchenOwl, without loading the recipe page.
- Categories (Starter, Main course, Dessert, Vegetarian, Vegan, Pasta, Soup, Salad, Baking, Breakfast) with a filter in the collection. A heart suggests categories from the tags, keywords and title of the recipe page; only these become tags in KitchenOwl (before: all keywords of the source). Categories can be changed in the collection.
- Hide recipes in the collection. They stay in KitchenOwl; "Show hidden" and a new heart bring them back. Existing favourites are kept when upgrading.
- New tab "Week plan" based on the KitchenOwl meal plan: plan recipes on a day, remove them, put the ingredients of the week on the shopping list.
- Calendar "Week plan" and sensor "Today" on the new service device "EatFresh". The meal plan is fetched every 15 minutes and after each change.
- Dashboard cards `custom:eatfresh-week-card` and `custom:eatfresh-today-card`, loaded on all dashboards.
- New WebSocket commands `eatfresh/collection/recipe`, `eatfresh/collection/hide`, `eatfresh/collection/unhide`, `eatfresh/collection/set_tags`, `eatfresh/tags`, `eatfresh/planner`, `eatfresh/planner/add`, `eatfresh/planner/remove`, `eatfresh/planner/shopping_list`. `eatfresh/collection` takes `include_hidden` and returns `tags` and `hidden`; `eatfresh/favorite` also returns `tags`.
- `eatfresh/planner` also returns `today` (time zone of Home Assistant). The panel and the cards use it for "today" and the current week instead of the browser's time zone.
- Planning a recipe that is in the plan without a day replaces that entry, as KitchenOwl does; the week plan shows this at once.
- Documented that the search limit applies per source.
- One recipe per page: links with or without `www.`, with tracking parameters (`utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid` and the like), `ref` or `share`, as AMP page, with an anchor, another scheme or percent encoding in the path count as the same address. Pasted links are saved under the canonical address of the page, but only if it names the same page (same host, path and recipe parameters); a canonical start or overview page is ignored. Favourites of 0.1.0 are still found.
- "Vegetarian" and "Vegan" are only suggested from a whole tag or the diet in the recipe data, never from the title, part of a tag or a tag with punctuation ("vegan?", "#vegan"). Pancakes and sandwiches are no longer "Baking". Canonical categories of both languages are one filter chip.
- Recipe ids outside the own collection are refused (`not_found`) before anything is sent to KitchenOwl and no longer start a reauthentication. Hiding accepts only recipes of the own collection.
- "Add the week's ingredients" remembers when a week was sent (for all devices) and asks before a second time (`confirm`, `already_added_at`, `shopping_list_at`). A recipe planned on two days counts twice. Two transfers of the same week at the same time send it only once; the second one is asked. After a partial transfer, a confirmed second try sends only the recipes that did not go through.
- Days are accepted from 2000 to 2100 only. A store with a broken part starts anyway, with a warning.
- `eatfresh/planner` answers the current week from the plan of the last 60 seconds, so many cards do not each ask KitchenOwl.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-15)

First release.

- Sidebar panel "EatFresh": search recipes, open a recipe, mark favourites and browse the collection.
- Recipe sources: Gaumenfreundin, Emmi kocht einfach, Küchengötter and any page with schema.org recipe data ("paste a recipe link"). Chefkoch is an opt-in source and is off by default.
- Favourites are saved as recipes in KitchenOwl with ingredients, steps, times, servings, source link and photo. A recipe is never created twice and nothing is ever deleted in KitchenOwl.
- Add the ingredients of saved recipes to a KitchenOwl shopping list.
- Actions `eatfresh.import_recipe` and `eatfresh.add_recipe_to_shopping_list`.
- Setup through the user interface, texts in German and English.
- Polite fetching: only on user action, one request per host at a time with a pause, cached results.
- Requests to addresses supplied by users only reach public hosts.
